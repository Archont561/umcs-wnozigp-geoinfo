applies to the rest of this package.

- **The mark.** `UMCS` and the eagle-and-rule device (`sygnet`) are the official
  visual identity of **Uniwersytet Marii Curie-Skłodowskiej w Lublinie**
  (Maria Curie-Skłodowska University, Lublin, Poland), Pl. M. Curie-Skłodowskiej

  rights reserved by the university.
- **What this file is.** A re-encoding of the shortened logotype that is
  embedded in the faculty's own title-page sample, cropped to its ink and
  downsampled for use at 86.6 mm width. Nothing was redrawn or recoloured. It
  is not a copy of the university's download archive and will not be
  byte-identical to any file in it.
- **Official source and rules.** The university publishes the logotype at

  is governed by the *Księga Identyfikacji Wizualnej UMCS* (2024, v02),
  <https://phavi.umcs.pl/at/attachments/2024/0726/085914-kiw-umcs-2024v02.pdf>.
  Use of the mark is subject to those rules and to the university's permission;
  this notice grants none.
- **Why it is here.** The Faculty of Earth Sciences and Spatial Management
  requires its title page to carry the mark. This package reproduces that
  required form.
- **Redistribution status.** Unresolved. See `README.md` in this directory and
