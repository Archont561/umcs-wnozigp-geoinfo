## Goal

Decide whether, where, and under what human gates a future package may be distributed. This milestone authorizes no publication by itself.

## Grouped tasks

| Task | Work |
| --- | --- |
| TASK-13 | Review destinations, ownership, licenses, assets, versioning, artifacts, checksums, and approval gates. |

## Exit criteria

A separate explicit decision records approved destination(s), account ownership, supported artifacts, immutable build/checksum policy, and independent human approvals. Until then, no publisher, credentials, registry submission, or CTAN upload is permitted.
