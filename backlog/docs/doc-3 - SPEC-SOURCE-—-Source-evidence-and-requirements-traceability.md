## Purpose

Prevent assumptions from becoming template behavior. This document is the evidence ledger for academic requirements. Every institutional claim added to LaTeX or Typst must have a source, edition, section/page, applicability note, and reviewer.

- **Review status:** Candidate source; TASK-1 must verify its currentness, exact sections, page numbers, and any amendments before implementation.

## Evidence rules

1. Prefer the current official faculty/university document over secondary summaries.
2. Record edition, publication or retrieval date, section and printed/PDF page where available.
3. Distinguish mandatory requirements, recommendations, examples, and maintainer choices.
4. Record conflicting sources and cohort dates; do not resolve conflicts silently.
5. Leave a requirement Open when the source is ambiguous. Do not claim compliance from a smoke PDF.
