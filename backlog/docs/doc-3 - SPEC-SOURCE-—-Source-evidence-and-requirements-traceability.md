## Evidence rules

1. Prefer the current official faculty/university document over secondary summaries.
2. Record edition, publication or retrieval date, and section locator; state why page numbers are unavailable when they are.
3. Distinguish mandatory requirements, recommendations, examples, and maintainer choices.
4. Record conflicting sources and cohort dates; do not resolve conflicts silently. The 2017 edition's divergent figures are recorded above and explicitly not used.
5. Leave a requirement Open when the source is ambiguous. Do not claim compliance from a smoke PDF.

## What this does not establish

- **Cohort applicability beyond the source's own statement.** The 2025/26 rules bind students who began thesis preparation in the 2025/26 summer semester. Whether they still bind a 2026/27 cohort, or whether a newer edition will be issued, is not confirmed by the source and remains an owner question (TASK-2).
- **Compliance.** Recording a rule is not evidence that any template implements it, and no template exists yet.
- **Institutional approval.** No reviewer has signed this ledger off.
