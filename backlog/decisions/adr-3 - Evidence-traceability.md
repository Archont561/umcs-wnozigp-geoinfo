# ADR-3: Trace academic requirements to dated evidence

## Context

The repository is described as tailored to WNoZiGP 2025/26 Geoinformatics guidance, but applicability to a future cohort and any amendments have not been verified.

## Decision

1. Record sources, edition, section/page, cohort applicability, and review state in SPEC-SOURCE.
2. Do not implement institutional requirements solely from memory, screenshots, or secondary summaries.
3. Keep ambiguous or conflicting requirements open until resolved with an authoritative source or faculty review.
4. State explicitly that draft specifications and smoke builds do not certify compliance or imply UMCS endorsement.

## Consequences

- The 2025/26 PDF is a candidate source, not a blanket current policy.
- TASK-1 and TASK-2 must be completed before format-specific academic behavior is implemented.
